The brand

A J and a V locked together, leaning forward. Everything on this site comes from that one shape.

One mark, one colour.

Clear space is one J stem on every side. The smallest size is 16 pixels tall. No container, no gradient, no outline.

Everything leans by 12°.

Corners stay sharp. The only curve is the one in the J. Two shapes sit close, with a thin gap between them.

A buttonAnother

Large, the mark is a window.

The J is flat colour. The V holds the portrait, printed in the same colour, and the print fades out at the collar.

Two grounds, two signals.

Press a colour to copy it. A project page swaps Blue for one accent of its own.

One family, one second hand.
